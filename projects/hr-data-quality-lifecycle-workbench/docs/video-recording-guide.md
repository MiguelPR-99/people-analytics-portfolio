# Demonstration recording guide

[← Case study](../README.md) · [Español](video-recording-guide.es.md)

Video is an optional publication enhancement. Use one **60–90 second** overview for recruiters; publish deeper technical walkthroughs separately only if they add value.

## Recommended 75-second story

| Time | Screen | Message |
|---:|---|---|
| 0–8 s | Title or scorecard | HR records can look valid separately and fail when reconciled; this workbench turns those differences into traceable work. |
| 8–22 s | DQ Command Center | Show the health indicators and filter one critical exception. Fabric detects; Power Apps prioritizes. |
| 22–40 s | Exception and case | Select a record and point to ownership, source correction, evidence, and approval readiness. |
| 40–55 s | Dataverse case | Show activity/evidence. Power Automate coordinates notifications, approval, and errors without changing the source HRIS. |
| 55–70 s | Power BI | Show quality, recurrence, published cases, and the distinction between resolution and confirmed source correction. |
| 70–75 s | Close | Synthetic data, human controls, and reproducible artifacts are available on GitHub. |

## Recording checklist

- Prepare one synthetic case and reset every filter.
- Close duplicate windows, messages, notifications, and unrelated tabs.
- Hide URLs, tenant details, email, avatar, credentials, tokens, authentication history, and personal paths.
- Keep one Power BI instance open and verify that every visual renders correctly.
- Record at 1920 × 1080, 30 fps, MP4/H.264, AAC audio, around 8–12 Mbps.
- Use slow cursor movement, consistent zoom, short narration, and subtitles.
- Rewatch the complete export before publishing.

If you want more depth, keep the short overview and add separate 2–3 minute clips for architecture/data, Power Apps/Power Automate, and Power BI. Avoid repeating the full story in every clip. For GitHub, keep the MP4 below 25 MB or link to a hosted version while retaining a higher-quality master outside the repository.
