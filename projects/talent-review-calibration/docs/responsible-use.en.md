## Purpose and data

This case shows how People Analytics can organize assessments to prepare Talent Review and calibration discussions. All employees, names, assignments, and reviews are synthetic. It contains no confidential information from a real organization and does not demonstrate business outcomes.

## Interpretation limits

- Performance and potential are organizational assessments involving human judgment. The dashboard receives those ratings.
- The 9-box matrix compresses complex information into broad categories. Its labels do not permanently define people.
- Movement describes rating changes between periods. It does not establish causes or demonstrate objective improvement or decline.
- Calibration signals compare averages with the selected population. They do not establish bias, discrimination, or management quality.
- The 0.25-point threshold and minimum of five employees are exercise rules with no established statistical validity.
- Talent Balance compares only Future Leader and Under Performer. It does not measure overall talent quality, attrition risk, or leadership readiness.
- Small groups can show large percentage changes from a single different assessment. Their results require context.

The screenshots retain terms such as *talent risk* and *pipeline strength*. They must be read alongside these limits and the [calculation rules](metric-dictionary.en.md).

## Human review

The report does not calculate individual promotion, compensation, succession, or termination recommendations. Any decision requires additional evidence, documented criteria, and human accountability.

A real implementation would also need role-based access, data limited to each function's needs, correction mechanisms, and a review of potentially unequal effects across groups. These are requirements to resolve before using the exercise in a real process; they are not presented as controls implemented in this demo.

## AI assistance

I used AI tools to explore design options and troubleshoot issues during development. I manually reviewed business rules, calculations, interactions, and outputs against the synthetic source data.

This is a learning project. AI assistance was part of its development; the rules and their limits should be explainable from the documentation and data.
